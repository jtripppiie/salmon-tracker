Salmon Tracker is an independent, unofficial Android client for selected public
Alaska Department of Fish and Game fish-count projects.

Follow Kenai, Kasilof, and Russian River salmon counts, compare the current run
with previous years, and receive a local notification when the official source
publishes or revises a count.

Features:

* Downloads counts directly from the public ADF&amp;G website
* Stores counts and preferences locally on your device
* Checks for updates in the background with user-controlled timing
* Supports quiet hours, project muting, and notification thresholds
* Shows seven-day, 14-day, and season views
* Compares the current run with up to five prior years
* Contains no advertising, analytics, accounts, or tracking SDKs

The ADF&amp;G source is public but is not a documented application API, so a
future source-format change may require an app update. Fish-passage counts are
historical context and do not guarantee fishing conditions or success.
